# where-the-ball-dev-edition
A lightweight handoff layer for small development teams. Catch the ball, throw the ball and catch it back because a to do list is made up of more than just one tick when you're collaborating. Do the work in GitHub/GitLab without keeping up the paperwork because it listens by itself.

## My Solution
A project to tell us where the ball is... collaborative to do list except it actually looks at where things are in github/gitlab.

## The problem
Honestly I got tired of navigating between MR pages and checking through and trying to see if and when people had fixed their mr so i could go back to it cos gitlab's internal my to do list sucks and the moment i review an mr it decides it is done from my to do list... it's NOT done it just means that it is not on my to do list for now but someone still needs to do it and it will come back in my to do list

And lets not forget the ever ending list of issues which tbh are easy to forget to manage cos they're just not in ur face

oh and how annoying it is to navigate between mrs from one repo to another

## The story of how I came across the problem

I was using GitLab self hosted with my teams. I know kanbans and jira and all that exists but honestly for a small team jira is pretty overkill and gahhh paperwor... in that i dont need a timeline and story points etc so the to do list ends up (partly) in my head but even with a team of 3-7 people it gets unmanageable and I dont need the complexity of jira but other tools are too simple and I want something that has a deeper integration with github/gitlab et voila - where the ball is born

A way to track where the ball is because its not just about being a to do list it is about keeping track of where the ball is. And fuck me I dont't want to have five clicks to get to the ball (eg go between MRs in frontend and backend repos). I just want to be on my to do list and it take me to the ball... now gitlab and github do have that except their tracking of where the ball is was not great as mentioned in the problem statement. 

Basically this is a lightweight handoff layer... its not full on project management with timelines and all that ish its just a who needs to do what next with a small message board so that stuff isn't all over the place in my whatsapp.
